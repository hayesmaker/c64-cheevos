## Road Runner (Commodore 64)

**Beep beep! Keep running, keep eating, and don't get caught.**

*Road Runner* is US Gold's 1987 Commodore 64 conversion of the 1985 Atari Games arcade machine, based on the Warner Bros cartoons. The C64 version was made by **Canvas**: programming by **Sean Townsend**, graphics by **Martin Calvert** and sound by **Fred Gray**. You are the Road Runner, sprinting down the desert highway with Wile E. Coyote right behind you.

**Key Features:**

*   **Cartoon Chase:** The Coyote never gives up. Stay ahead of him and he goes home empty handed again.
*   **Birdseed:** Piles of seed line the road. Eat them for points, and to keep your strength up.
*   **Traffic:** Trucks thunder along the highway. They are as dangerous to you as they are to the Coyote.
*   **Acme Gadgets:** Expect the Coyote to try a few tricks of his own as the chase goes on.
*   **Five Lives:** You start with five lives and earn more as your score climbs.

**Gameplay:**

The road scrolls as you run left. Grab the birdseed, dodge the trucks and other hazards, and keep out of the Coyote's reach. Each time he catches you, you lose a life. The game ends when your last life is gone.

**Why Play *Road Runner*?**

It is a simple and fast chase game with bright cartoon graphics, and a good test of nerve. Leave it as late as you can before running from the Coyote, and steer him into the traffic.
