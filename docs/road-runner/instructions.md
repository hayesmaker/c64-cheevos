## Your Mission

You are the Road Runner. Run along the desert highway, eat as much birdseed as you can, and stay out of the clutches of Wile E. Coyote. Watch out for the trucks on the road and anything else the Coyote throws at you.

---

## Controls

Use a joystick in **port 2**.

| Input | Action |
|-------|--------|
| **Joystick** | Run in eight directions |
| **Fire** | Jump. Also starts the game |

---

## Starting a Game

Press fire on the title screen to start. The game sometimes takes a moment to respond, and you may need to press fire a second time.

---

## Lives

You start with **5 lives**, shown at the top left of the screen. You lose a life each time the Coyote catches you or you are hit by a hazard. When the last life is gone, the game is over.

---

## Scoring

| Points | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| 10 | Pile of birdseed |

You get an extra life at **30,000**, **60,000** and **90,000** points, and then each time the ten thousands digit reaches 3, 6 or 9 again (130,000, 160,000 and so on).

---

## High Score

The best score is shown at the top of the title screen, and it is saved to the cartridge, so it is still there next time you play. Press **R** on the boot screen to reset it.
