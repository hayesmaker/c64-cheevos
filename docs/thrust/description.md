## Thrust (Commodore 64)

**Steal the pod. Mind the gravity. Don't run out of fuel.**

*Thrust* is a gravity-defying cave shooter released by **Firebird** in 1986. It was written by **Jeremy C. Smith**, with a classic title tune by **Rob Hubbard**. You pilot a small rocket ship down into planetary caves to steal Klystron pods for the Resistance, towing each one back out to space on the end of a tractor beam while gun turrets do their best to shoot you down.

**Key Features:**

*   **Real Physics:** Your ship spins, thrusts and falls under gravity with real momentum. Every planet pulls with a different strength.
*   **Tow the Pod:** Once the pod is on your tractor beam it swings from your ship on a rigid tether, and its weight drags you around. Taming the swing is the whole game.
*   **Fuel Management:** Thrusting, shields and the tractor beam all burn fuel. Run dry and the game is over, however many lives you have left.
*   **Blow Up the Planet:** Shoot the reactor enough times and it goes critical. Escape with the pod before the planet explodes for a much bigger bonus.
*   **Six Caves, and Then Some:** Clear all six planets and they come round again with reverse gravity, then with invisible cave walls, then with both.
*   **Rob Hubbard Soundtrack:** The title music is one of the best known tunes on the C64.

**Gameplay:**

Each mission starts at the top of a cave. Fly down, shoot or dodge the gun turrets, top up your fuel from the fuel tanks, and find the pod sitting on its stand. Hover over it with the tractor beam to lock on, then fly back up and out into space with it swinging below you. Hit the cave walls with the ship or the pod and you lose a life.

**Why Play *Thrust*?**

*Thrust* is easy to learn and very hard to master. The controls are simple, but flying with a swinging pod on a tether through narrow caves takes real skill, and every planet feels different. 2026 marks 40 years since its release, and it is still one of the purest tests of piloting on the C64.
