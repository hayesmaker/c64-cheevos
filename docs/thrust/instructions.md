## Your Mission

The Intergalactic Empire is preparing for war against the Resistance, and the Resistance needs Klystron pods to power its fleet. The pods are kept deep inside caves on the Empire's planets, guarded by limpet gun turrets.

Your job is to fly down into each cave, lock on to the pod with your tractor beam, and tow it back up and out into space. You start with 3 spare lives. You lose a life if your ship or the pod hits the cave walls, or if you are shot. The game ends when you lose your last life or run out of fuel.

## Controls

| Key | Action |
|-----|--------|
| **A** | Rotate left |
| **S** | Rotate right |
| **SHIFT** | Thrust |
| **RETURN** | Fire |
| **SPACE** | Shield and tractor beam (also starts the game) |
| **F5** / **F7** | Pause / resume |
| **F1** / **F3** | Sound off / on |
| **RUN/STOP** | Quit the game |

## Flying Your Ship

Your ship has one engine. Rotate to point it where you want to go, then thrust. Gravity is always pulling you down, and your ship keeps its momentum, so small, short bursts of thrust work better than holding it down.

**Shield:** holding SPACE raises your shield. It protects you from turret fire, but not from the cave walls, and it uses fuel.

**Tractor beam:** SPACE also works the tractor beam. Hover just above the pod and hold SPACE to lock on. A line appears between your ship and the pod, and from then on the pod swings below you. Its weight pulls you around, so turn and thrust gently. Fly out of the top of the cave with the pod to complete the mission.

---

## Fuel

You start with 1000 units of fuel. Thrust, the shield and the tractor beam all use it. To refuel, hover above a fuel tank and hold SPACE: the tractor beam draws the fuel into your ship. Each tank can only be drained once.

If your fuel runs out, the game is over, even if you still have lives left.

---

## The Reactor

Each cave has a reactor powering the turrets. Shoot it and the turrets stop firing for a short time while it recovers. The damage builds up, though: shoot the reactor enough times and it goes critical, and a countdown starts. When it reaches zero the planet is destroyed.

Escape with the pod before the countdown ends and you earn a much bigger mission bonus. Still be in the cave when the planet explodes and the mission fails.

---

## Planets and Gravity

There are six planets, each with its own cave and gravity. Some caves have doors that are opened by shooting a switch. After the sixth planet the caves come round again, but harder:

1. Normal gravity
2. **Reverse gravity**: you fall upwards
3. **Invisible landscape**: the cave walls cannot be seen
4. Reverse gravity and invisible landscape together

The turrets also fire more often the more missions you complete.

## Scoring

| Points | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| 750 | Gun turret destroyed |
| 300 | Fuel tank collected |
| 150 | Fuel tank shot (destroyed instead of collected) |
| 400 per step | Mission bonus: the higher the planet, the more steps. Destroying the planet as you escape adds 5 more steps |

You get an extra life every 10,000 points.
