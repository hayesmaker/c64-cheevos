## Your Mission

The city of Xenon has fallen to an alien invasion. The population has been evacuated, almost all of it. A few people, mostly military personnel, are still hiding in bunkers in the city and the surrounding areas, and you are the only pilot left who can fly a Panther ground attack craft.

Fly from Panther base across the landscape, shoot down the invaders, pick up the survivors and get them to the spaceport on the far side of the city. Watch the message line on the cockpit display for warnings and new attack waves.

---

## Controls

Use a joystick in **port 2**. The controls work like an aircraft's: pull back to climb.

| Input | Action |
|-------|--------|
| **Joystick down** | Climb |
| **Joystick up** | Descend and land |
| **Joystick left / right** | Steer |
| **Fire** | Shoot. Also starts a new game on the game over screen |
| **F7** | Pause / continue |
| **F1** or **RESTORE** | Abandon the current game and start again (the score is not submitted) |

Watch the shadows of your Panther and the aliens. They show your height and where everything is relative to you.

---

## Starting a Game

The game starts on its own a few seconds after it has finished loading, so be ready with the joystick. After a game over, press fire to play again.

---

## Rescuing Survivors

Land close to a bunker and the survivors come out to your craft. Stay on the ground until they are all on board, whatever the aliens are doing, then take off and move on as quickly as you can. When you reach the end of your run, find the way to get your passengers to safety and land to drop them off.

It may take more than one run to clear the city. The transmat sends you back to Panther base, ready to go and get more people.

---

## The Landscape

You start from the base in the desert and head in towards the city centre, which stands on an island in a large inland lake. The lake has oil platforms where more survivors may be hiding.

Things get much tougher near the city. Surface-to-air missile batteries hide among the buildings, and if you don't obey the flight computer when it tells you to **FLY UNDER RADAR**, they hunt you down quickly. That means flying around the skyscrapers, not over them. The spaceport beyond the city is surrounded by alien siege craft.

---

## Lives

You start with **6**, shown on the right of the cockpit display: your Panther and five Phoenix shields. Each time you crash or are shot down, a Phoenix shield repairs the craft (`PHEONIX MANUEVER COMPLETE`) and the count drops by one. Dropping off survivors can earn you an extra one. When the count reaches zero, the game is over.

---

## Scoring

| Points | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| 100 | Enemy shot down |
| 500 | Survivor picked up |
| 1,500 | Each survivor dropped off after landing |

The big points come from getting survivors safely away, so don't just stick to shooting.

---

## High Score

The best score of the session is shown on the game over screen. It is reset when the machine is switched off.
